# Local Bonsai extensions

Bonsai compiles every `*.cs` file here when `src\main.bonsai` loads, as C# 5 (`src\Extensions.csproj` sets `LangVersion` 5): no `$"..."`, `?.`, `nameof`, expression-bodied members or property initialisers. `tests\Extensions.Tests` (MSTest, net472, C# 5) tests them; `scripts\check-workflow.ps1` compiles and runs both. Everything shared between dome workflows lives in lib-open-rigs (see the README's Shared Blocks); only this task's logic is here.

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `TrialLogic.cs` | `Trial` (lib-open-rigs' `ConditionedTrial`, the shared columns' fields, and `ILaserDurationTrial`, its combination's `optogenetics.laserDurationSeconds`; its own `target`, `singleSpoutOffered`, `repeatCount`, `visualInitialAzimuthDeg`, the grating's start `gratingAzimuthDeg`, `auditorySoundFileIndex`, a random trial's coin; `VisualStimulus` and `SpoutsStayIn` read from its combination); `TrialDraw` (from the task file's text: each of `trialConditions` built at Go by writing its `set` over a copy of the text at its dotted paths, once per combination (its `pairs` groups' lists zipped, every other list cell one pick, a list field's entry a one-entry list), every list field's entry a combination too, at most `TaskSchema.MaxCombinations` (1024), each combination checked as a task; per trial a condition by weight, one combination uniformly, whose values the trial reads (timing, grating, response rule, penalty, spouts, laser), logged as `trialConditionId` and `trialCombinationId` (its row of `Settings_Combinations`, `TrialRules.CombinationsJson`, written at Go), its `trialType` (derived at Go from its values by the schema's `x-trial-types`, never stored) as `trialType`; `TaskSchema` (generated with the schema) its `x-session-fields`, `x-max-combinations`, list fields and `x-trial-types`; the grating at a contrast (0: none) from `visualStimulus.initialAzimuthsDeg`, the sound at `auditoryStimulus.levelDbfs` (`mute`: none, NaN) on rig.yml's speaker nearest a pick of `auditoryStimulus.initialAzimuthsDeg` at `auditoryStimulus.elevationDeg`, the target from the condition's `target` (`visualSide`, `auditorySide`: the picked azimuth's side); `singleSpoutOffered` in go/no-go always, else on a scored trial whose spouts move at `singleSpoutProbability`; repeats by the combination's `repeats.maxRepeats` and `repeatOn` (a probe only on `noResponse` after a miss), never with the laser; random-reward probes, the Zapit condition, each trial's own interval; `MaxStimulusLevel` and `MaxPenaltyLevel` (each a `LoudestSound`) the highest stimulus and penalty level any weighted condition can draw; refuses a `curriculum` empty or malformed, a task-level `target` or a condition without one, a probe repeating on `notCorrect`, a set or paired session field (or `optogenetics.*` with optogenetics off), unequal paired lists, more than 1024 combinations, a spouts-out move, grating or laser that overruns another combination's interval, empty or negative contrasts, a level that is neither a number nor `mute` or is above -1 dBFS, a grating azimuth of 0, a sound azimuth whose nearest speaker is not on its side or 0 with target `auditorySide`, and the timing rules: `laserToStimulusSeconds` + 0.2 s within `itiMinSeconds` and no later than the response window, `itiMinSeconds` at least `outcomeToSpoutsOutSeconds`, the quiet waits, and with optogenetics `laserDurationSeconds` at most `responseDelaySeconds` + `itiMinSeconds` - 0.5 s ramp-down - 0.2 s); `TrialRules` (lib-open-rigs' `ITrialRules`, which its `TrialLoop` runs: the draw, each trial's timing and cues `GratingOff`, `SpoutsIn` and `SpoutsOut`, the laser as `LaserTiming.ForDuration`, `LoudestPenaltyLevelDbfs` for `PlayPenaltySound`'s buffer, and the response rule, the decision variable and grating moved `azimuthShiftPerLickDeg` per lick as `DecisionUpdate`, the outcome as `TrialOutcome`: the row, the reward, the spouts, the penalty sound at the trial's level and the timeout); `ReadTask` (the task file's text into `TaskConfig`, as the generated `DeserializeFromYaml` but with a condition's `set` values typed, so Settings_Task logs numbers); `CheckTaskAtGo` (the task file's text, `TaskText`, checked once at Go into `TrialRules`); `RunTrials` (`TrialLoop.Observe` on the workflow's streams); `TargetWords` (every word the target column holds: `left`, `right`, `go`, `noGo`, `noReward`, `random`; their outcome is lib-open-rigs' `TrialOutcomes.Probe`); `Choice` (the choice column's values: `left`, `right`, `go`, `noResponse`); `DecisionUpdate` (the grating's azimuth after a lick); `TrialOutcome` (lib-open-rigs' `TrialOutcome<TrialRow>`, the reward at the valve `Of` names: the row, the reward, the penalty sound at the trial's `penalty.sound.levelDbfs`, the timeout; and the spouts it moves, none when the trial's spouts stay in); `SpoutCommands` (every spout move from the trial events into `LickSpoutStage.Commands`: the spouts out at trial 1's interval start and at each `SpoutsOut` cue unless the coming trial's spouts stay in (the loop draws it at its interval's start, before the cue), the outcome's unless this trial's stay in; at each `SpoutsIn` cue both in, or the target's alone on a `singleSpoutOffered` trial) |
