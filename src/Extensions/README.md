# Local Bonsai extensions

Bonsai compiles every `*.cs` file here when `src\main.bonsai` loads, as C# 5 (`src\Extensions.csproj` sets `LangVersion` 5): no `$"..."`, `?.`, `nameof`, expression-bodied members or property initialisers. `tests\Extensions.Tests` (MSTest, net472, C# 5) tests them; `scripts\check-workflow.ps1` compiles and runs both. Everything shared between dome workflows lives in lib-open-rigs (see the README's Shared Blocks); only this task's logic is here.

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `TrialLogic.cs` | `Trial` (lib-open-rigs' `IZapitTrial`, for its `ZapitTrialCommand`); `TrialDraw` (from the task file's text: each of `trialConditions` built at Go by writing its `set` over a copy of the text at its dotted paths, once per combination (its `pairs` groups' lists zipped, every other list cell one pick, a list field's entry a one-entry list), every list field's entry a combination too, at most `TaskSchema.MaxCombinations` (1024), each combination checked as a task; per trial a condition by weight, one combination uniformly, whose values the trial reads (timing, grating, response rule, penalty), logged as `trialCondition` and `trialCombinationId` (its row of `Settings_Combinations`, `TrialRules.CombinationsJson`, written at Go), its `trialType` as `trialType`; `TaskSchema` (generated with the schema) its `x-session-fields`, `x-max-combinations` and list fields; the grating at a contrast (0: none) from `visualStimulus.initialAzimuthsDeg`, the sound at `auditoryStimulus.amplitude` (0: none) on rig.yml's speaker nearest a pick of `auditoryStimulus.initialAzimuthsDeg`, the target from the condition's `target` (`visualSide`, `auditorySide`: the picked azimuth's side); repeats by the combination's `repeats.maxRepeats` and `repeatOn`, random-reward probes, the Zapit condition, each trial's own interval; `Loudest` (a `LoudestSound`) the largest amplitude any weighted condition can draw; refuses a `curriculum` empty or malformed, a set or paired session field (or `optogenetics.*` with optogenetics off), unequal paired lists, more than 1024 combinations, a drink, grating or laser that overruns another combination's interval, empty or negative contrasts, a grating azimuth of 0, a sound azimuth whose nearest speaker is not on its side or 0 with target `auditorySide`, and the timing rules: `laserToStimulusSeconds` + 0.2 s within `itiMinSeconds` and no later than the response window, `itiMinSeconds` at least `drinkSeconds`, the quiet waits, and with optogenetics `laserDurationSeconds` at most `responseDelaySeconds` + `itiMinSeconds` - 0.5 s ramp-down - 0.2 s); `TrialRules` (lib-open-rigs' `ITrialRules`, which its `TrialLoop` runs: the draw, each trial's timing and cues `GratingOff`, `SpoutsIn` and `Drink`, the laser as `LaserTiming.ForDuration`, and the response rule, the decision variable and grating moved per lick as `DecisionUpdate`, the outcome as `TrialOutcome`: the row, the reward, the spouts, the penalty sound and the timeout); `ReadTask` (the task file's text into `TaskConfig` and `LaunchTaskConfig`, as the generated `DeserializeFromYaml` but with a condition's `set` values typed, so Settings_Task logs numbers); `CheckTaskAtGo` (the task file's text, `TaskText`, checked once at Go into `TrialRules`); `TrialConditionDraw` and `TrialConditionCombination` (a condition as built at Go and one combination of its list cells); `RunTrials` (`TrialLoop.Observe` on the workflow's streams); `Target` (the target's values other than a side: `Go`, `NoGo`, `NoReward`, `Random`; their outcome is lib-open-rigs' `TrialOutcomes.Probe`); `Choice` (the choice column's values: `Left`, `Right`, `Lick`, `NoResponse`); `DecisionUpdate` (the grating's azimuth after a lick); `TrialOutcome` (lib-open-rigs' `TrialOutcome<TrialRow>`, the reward at the valve `Of` names: the row, the reward, the penalty sound, the timeout; and the spouts it moves); `SpoutCommands` (every spout move from the trial events into `LickSpoutStage.Commands`: with spout movements, the spouts out at trial 1's interval start and at each `Drink` cue, and the outcome's; at each `SpoutsIn` cue both in on a both-spouts trial, else the target's) |
| `TrialRow.cs` | `TrialRow`, one `WorkflowData_Trials` row, its fields the columns of `schemas\trial-columns.yml` in order (a test holds them equal), built at the outcome from the trial and the loop's `TrialTimes` |
| `AuditoryStimulusSet.cs` | The stimulus set (`AuditoryStimulusSet`, read at Start by `LoadAuditoryStimulusSet`), each waveform equalised for each speaker by its filter, checked at Go for the loudest amplitude the conditions can draw (`LevelAuditoryStimulusSet` on `TrialRules.Loudest`, every equalised copy's peak under -1 dBFS, a failure naming the condition and speaker), and a trial's mixer buffer at its `auditoryAmplitude` on its speaker or, at `"all"`, every speaker, with its sync copy (`SelectAuditoryStimulus`, OpenRigs.Audio `SpeakerSet.Layout`); refuses a speaker with no filter, a filter from another machine or rate, and any missing or malformed file, naming it |
