# coen-dome-decision-making

The audiovisual dome decision-making tasks (localization and two modality discriminations, go/no-go and left/right), as an open-rigs project on the lab's shared blocks (lib-open-rigs, [Building Blocks](https://github.com/Coen-Lab/lib-open-rigs/blob/main/docs/articles/building-blocks.md)). It was `ucl-open/av-dome`'s `pip/main-switchover` branch, moved here with its history so production av-dome is never touched. A mouse's experiments recorded as av-dome's switch-over branch before the move count as this workflow's: anything that reads a mouse's history (a stage suggestion, for one) reads both. Only the current format is read: production av-dome's TrialSettings logs, task files without `taskType` and folders named by eartag are not (their readers were deleted; migrating every mouse's past sessions is planned in its own session).

The rig's hardware is in `C:\Coen-Lab-Local\RigConfigs\<MACHINE-NAME>\rig.yml`, the lab rig model `open_rigs.rigs.lab_rig.LabRig` (start from open-rigs' `rigs\rig-template.yml`).

## Running an experiment

The workflow has no window of its own. app-rig-control launches it with `--property:SubjectId=<lab ID> --property:SessionId=<n>`, and it starts into ready mode: the boards connected, the spouts homed and at that mouse's saved positions, the cameras streaming to the live view, nothing recorded. Set the parameters, send commands, then Go.

| Parameter | Default | |
|-----------|---------|-|
| `SpoutStepSize` | 150 | Motor steps per spout nudge, taken live; declared by DomeHardware's `LickSpoutStage` itself |

The task's own settings are the task file's: app-rig-control writes the file chosen at launch from `config\task*.yml`, with the Experiment page's edits, checked against `src\DataSchemas\ucl_open_coen_dome_decision_making_task.json`, at the path it passes as `TaskPath`, read at Go (it writes the chosen file there at launch too, which the workflow reads at Start for the background noise). From the editor `TaskPath` is `config\task-localization.yml`. `taskType` is `localization` (the answer is a side), `discriminationGoNoGo` (one spout, the left: lick on a `go` trial, withhold on a `noGo` one; a miss has no penalty; Song et al. 2017) or `discriminationLeftRight` (the left spout on a `left` trial, the right on a `right` one). The training stages are the share's Mouse Curriculum (`K:\config\curricula\coen-dome-decision-making\<task file stem>.conditions.yml`, edited on app-rig-control's Mouse Curriculum page): at launch app-rig-control writes the chosen stage's values over the task file's (`trialConditions` set whole; a field the stage leaves unset keeps the file's) and fills `curriculum` (`id`, `code`, `version`, `path`, `label`, `sha256`), the only record of the stage (the workflow has no stage parameter): Go refuses a `curriculum` that is empty or malformed, and `TaskFile` reports its `code-version` (S03A-k7f2) as the metric `trainingStage`. The repo's task files hold the defaults (the first stage's values) and an empty `curriculum`. The trial mix is `trialConditions`, each `{label, weight, pairs, set}`: each trial picks a condition by weight and draws the task's values with its `set` over them. What a trial shows and rewards are its settings, never its label: `contrasts` (0 no grating), `auditoryStimulus.amplitude` (0 no sound, 1 the WAV as stored) at a pick of `auditoryStimulus.startAzimuthsDeg` (0 the centre), `target` (`visualSide`, `auditorySide`, `left`, `right`, `go`, `noGo`, `random`, `noReward`) and `repeats.maxRepeats`/`repeatOn`; `pairs` makes lists share one pick (coherent: the grating and sound azimuths together; conflict: mirrored). `label` names the condition (logged as `trialType`). The schema's `x-conditionFields` lists what a condition may set and `x-rules` the rules linking them (a random target needs both spouts and never repeats; a task type allows only its targets), checked by pydantic and at Go. A trial with no right answer is a probe (outcome `Probe`, left out of performance) and never repeats: target `noReward` in go/no-go (never rewarded or penalised), target `random` in localization and left-right: both spouts move in and a choice at either is rewarded there or penalised as an incorrect choice on a coin drawn with the trial from the seeded stream, a miss as a scored trial's miss. A go/no-go task with no weighted `go` target, or with `bothSpoutsProbability` above 0, is refused.

Commands, while ready or recording: `RewardValve-Left.Reward` and `RewardValve-Right.Reward`, each 2.5 µl on that spout through the valve's calibration (a fixed manual drop, each `RewardValve` piece's), and `LickSpoutStage`'s own (lib-open-rigs'): `SpoutsHome`, `SpoutsIn`, `SpoutsOut`, `SaveSpoutPositions` (the mouse's row of `RigConfigs\<RIG>\LickSpoutPositions.csv`, beside rig.yml) and the nudges `LeftUp`, `LeftDown`, `RightUp`, `RightDown`, `LeftIn`, `LeftOut`, `RightIn`, `RightOut`, `BaseLeft`, `BaseRight`; a nudge moves the spouts at once and changes that mouse's in positions, which `SaveSpoutPositions` keeps for its next experiment.

## A trial

The trial is one state machine on the Harp clock, lib-open-rigs' `TrialLoop` (the same as coen-dome-navigation's), running this task's tested C# rules (`TrialRules` in `TrialLogic.cs`); `TaskLogic` sends each of its events to the hardware. Go reads the task file and checks it once (`CheckTaskAtGo`, in `TaskFile`, building `TrialRules`: a well formed `curriculum`, the weights and repeat rules keyed for the task type and a go/no-go stage with Go trials, the interval at least `drinkSeconds`, `laserToStimulusSeconds` within the shortest interval less Zapit's 0.2 s reply margin (`ZapitTrial.ReplyMarginSeconds`) and no later than the response window, the quiet waits, and with optogenetics a `laserDurationSeconds` short enough for Zapit to be armed for the next trial, and the conditions file), stopping the workflow naming each problem; once the experiment folder exists the spouts are drawn back (on a stage with spout movements) and the loop starts (`RunTrials`), the first trial's interval starting then. Each trial is drawn at the start of its interval, the last outcome plus its timeout (`TrialDraw`: type by the stage's weights or the task's, by role in the discriminations, repeats by the rules, the speaker that plays is rig.yml's nearest to the drawn location, its own `itiSeconds`, and with optogenetics a Zapit condition on every trial, the laser off at 0 mW on laser-off trials). It starts at the interval's end less the lead, max(`laserToStimulusSeconds`, 0), or later until the quiet waits hold (`trialStructure.lickQuietSeconds` with both spouts released, a held contact holding the start; the wheel's travel, forward and back added, within `wheelQuietCm` over the last `wheelQuietSeconds`; each counted from the interval's start; all 0, off, in the task files); `Trial` and the status metrics change then. The stimulus comes the lead after the trial start, in every experiment, so with a quiet mouse it lands exactly `itiSeconds` after the interval's start (`quietWaitSeconds` says how much the waits added). With optogenetics, `Zapit` sends a hardware-triggered Zapit the trial's StartStimulus during the interval (`Arm`, or once the last trial's laser and its 0.5 s longest ramp-down have ended; a rig without hardware triggering, test mouse only, gets it at the trigger, as Zapit starts the laser on receipt), the loop checks the reply, and the ZapitTrigger line (Zapit's trigger and the masking light) rises at the stimulus less `laserToStimulusSeconds` (signed: before, with or after the stimulus) and falls `laserDurationSeconds` later, unless the trial was answered first (no laser, and the row logs `laserPowerMw` 0); on a hardware-triggered rig a reply not in by then stops the workflow, so Zapit adds no time. The stimulus (`stimulusOnsetTime`, the first clock tick at or after it was due), the grating off `stimulusSeconds` after it (`GratingOff`) and the spouts in `stimulusToSpoutsInSeconds` after it (`SpoutsIn`, dropped when the outcome comes first); the response window `responseDelaySeconds` after it for `responseWindowSeconds`: each lick in it adds 1 (left) or -1 (right) to the net licks, the grating moving `degreesPerLick` a lick from its drawn azimuth, and the choice is Left at `thresholdLicks`, Right at -`thresholdLicks`, else NoResponse at the window's end (in `discriminationGoNoGo` only left licks count and the choice is Lick at `thresholdLicks`); a lick before the window is not counted. The answer is the trial's target: Left or Right, in go/no-go Go or NoGo, or on a probe NoReward or Random (`schemas\trial-columns.yml`). At the outcome the trial's row is built and its consequence runs from the row (its `rewarded`: `rewardOfferedUl` at the target, or a Random trial's chosen spout; spouts; its `penalised`: the lab's penalty sound and the timeout); then the timeout, and the next interval from its end (`itiStartTime`), the spouts out `drinkSeconds` into it (`Drink`).

uv run pytest

`check-workflow.ps1` needs `.bonsai\Setup.ps1` run once (Bonsai and its packages): it compiles the Extensions under C# 5, runs `tests\Extensions.Tests`, builds `src\main.bonsai` with Bonsai's own builders against the template filled in as a rig's (no window, no device), and reads each task file as at Go.

## Data

The experiment folder is `<rootPath>\sub-<SubjectId>\ses-<SessionId>_<DateTime>`, with a folder per device, `Settings` (`Settings_Rig_`, `Settings_Task_`, `Settings_Experiment_`), each valve's calibration under `RewardValve-Left\` and `RewardValve-Right\`, `WorkflowData` and `manifest.json`. `WorkflowData_Trials` has one row per trial, written when the outcome is known (columns and meanings in `schemas\trial-columns.yml`); `WorkflowData_Events` one row per event outside a trial (commands, Go, each reward as `RewardDelivered` with the microlitres delivered). Status reports `trialNumber`, `trainingStage`, `target`, `trialType`, the azimuths, `rewardOfferedUl` and `laserCondition`; app-rig-control computes trials, performance and water from the files. Each camera's folder (`Camera-Face`, `Camera-Pupil`, `Camera-Body`) holds an hourly `.mkv` (H.264) with a `.csv` of one row per frame: `harpTime`, `frameId`, `cameraClockNs`, `exposureUs`, `gainDb` (`LogVideoFfmpeg`; the encoder is `videoEncoder` in rig.yml).

Every file's first column is `harpTime`, the row's Harp time; a trial's moments are their own columns, in the block of columns every dome task shares (lib-open-rigs' `open_rigs.rigs.trials`, held by `tests\test_trial_columns.py`). `uv run summarise-experiment <experiment folder>` reads one experiment from its files alone: how the run ended, trials, performance, each response time and outcome-to-next-stimulus interval, and the water asked against the water delivered. It names each problem, exiting 1: trial numbers with a gap, an outcome not `Correct`, `Incorrect`, `NoResponse` or `Probe`, a trial start or a stimulus more than 2 ms from where the task file puts it, a laser trigger more than 2 ms from `laserToStimulusSeconds` before the stimulus, an interval not starting at the last outcome plus its `penaltyTimeoutSeconds`, a `penalised` trial without the task's timeout and, when the task has it on, the penalty sound (or an unpenalised one with either), a `rewarded` trial with no `RewardDelivered` at its outcome requesting its `rewardOfferedUl`, or a `RewardDelivered` at the outcome of a trial not rewarded.
