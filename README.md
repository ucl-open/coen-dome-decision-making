# coen-dome-decision-making

The audiovisual dome decision-making tasks (localisation and discrimination), as an open-rigs project on the lab's shared blocks (lib-open-rigs, [Building Blocks](https://github.com/Coen-Lab/lib-open-rigs/blob/main/docs/articles/building-blocks.md)). It was `ucl-open/av-dome`'s `pip/main-switchover` branch until 13 Nov 2026, moved here with its history (Pip) so production av-dome is never touched. A mouse's experiments recorded as av-dome before the move count as this workflow's: anything that reads a mouse's history (a stage suggestion, for one) reads both.

The workflow is `src\main.bonsai` in the Bonsai environment under `.bonsai\`, the task's C# in `src\Extensions\` (tested in `tests\Extensions.Tests`), the task files in `config\`, and the rig's hardware in `C:\Coen-Lab-Local\RigConfigs\<MACHINE-NAME>\rig.yml`, the lab rig model `open_rigs.rigs.lab_rig.LabRig` (start from open-rigs' `rigs\rig-template.yml`).

## Running an experiment

The workflow has no window of its own. app-rig-control launches it with `--property:SubjectId=<lab ID> --property:SessionId=<n>`, and it starts into ready mode: the boards connected, the spouts homed and at that mouse's saved positions, the cameras streaming to the live view, nothing recorded. Set the parameters, send commands, then Go.

| Parameter | Default | |
|-----------|---------|-|
| `TrainingStage` | 4 | Row of the task file's `trainingStages`, 0 to 4; a row past the end of the file's table stops the workflow at Go |
| `SpoutStepSize` | 150 | Motor steps per spout nudge, taken live |

The task's own settings are the task file's: app-rig-control writes the file chosen at launch from `config\task*.yml`, with the Experiment page's edits, checked against `src\DataSchemas\ucl_open_coen_dome_decision_making_task.json`, at the path it passes as `TaskPath`, read at Go. From the editor `TaskPath` is `config\task-localization.yml`. `trialWeights` and `repeats.rulesPerType` name each trial type by what it shows (`visual`, `auditory`, `audiovisualCoherent`, `audiovisualConflict`, `neutral`). In discrimination the task files' rows are written for a visual-target mouse (visual trials are the target trials): for an auditory-target mouse, swap the stage's `visual` and `auditory` weights on the form. A discrimination stage that gives the target modality no trials while the other has some is refused at Go, saying so.

Commands, while ready or recording: `RewardLeft` and `RewardRight`, each 2.5 µl on that spout through the valve's calibration (Pip's fixed manual drop), and the stage's own (`HarpLickSpoutStage`): `SpoutsHome`, `SpoutsIn`, `SpoutsOut`, `SaveSpoutPositions` (to `<rig config>\calibration\LickSpoutPositions\<SubjectId>.csv`) and the nudges `LeftUp`, `LeftDown`, `RightUp`, `RightDown`, `LeftFront`, `LeftBack`, `RightFront`, `RightBack`, `BaseLeft`, `BaseRight`; a nudge moves the spouts at once and is saved for that mouse.

## A trial

Every wait is on the Harp clock (Core `HarpDelay` on `Timestamp`); the trial's arithmetic is tested C# (`TrialLogic.cs`). Go reads the task file and checks it (`CheckTaskAtGo`: the stage in the table, the target modality given trials, the interval at least `drinkSeconds`, `laserToStimulusSeconds` within the shortest interval less Zapit's 0.2 s reply margin (`ZapitTrial.ReplyMarginSeconds`) and no later than the response window, the quiet waits, and with optogenetics a `laserDurationSeconds` short enough for Zapit to be armed for the next trial), stopping the workflow naming each problem; once the experiment folder exists the spouts go out (on a stage with spout movements) and the first trial is drawn, its interval starting then. Each trial is drawn at the start of its interval, the last outcome after any timeout (`DrawTrial`: type by the stage's weights, repeats by its rules, the speaker that plays is rig.yml's nearest to the drawn location, its own `itiSeconds`, and with optogenetics a Zapit condition on every trial, the laser off at 0 mW on laser-off trials). `WaitForGo` holds it until Go: the interval's end less the lead, max(`laserToStimulusSeconds`, 0), or later until the stage's quiet waits hold (`lickQuietSeconds` with both spouts released, a held contact holding Go; the wheel's travel, forward and back added, within `wheelQuietCm` over the last `wheelQuietSeconds`; each counted from the interval's start; all 0, off, in the task files). The stimulus comes the lead after Go (`LeadToStimulus`), in every experiment, so with a quiet mouse it lands exactly `itiSeconds` after the interval's start (`quietWaitSeconds` says how much the waits added). With optogenetics, `Laser` sends a hardware-triggered Zapit the trial's StartStimulus during the interval (or once the last trial's laser and its 0.5 s longest ramp-down have ended; a rig without hardware triggering, test mouse only, gets it at the trigger, as Zapit starts the laser on receipt), checks the reply, and raises the ZapitTrigger line (Zapit's trigger and the masking light, cleared `laserDurationSeconds` later) at the stimulus less `laserToStimulusSeconds` (signed: before, with or after the stimulus), unless the trial was answered first (`UnlessAnswered`: no laser, and the row logs `laserPowerMw` 0; Pip, 13 Nov 2026; the trial records which of its trigger and its outcome came first, and both read that record); on a hardware-triggered rig a reply not in by then stops the workflow (`RequireZapitArmed`), so Zapit adds no time. The stimulus (`stimulusOnsetTime`), the grating off after `stimulusSeconds`, the spouts in after `stimulusToSpoutsInSeconds`, the response window after `openLoopSeconds` for `closedLoopSeconds`. At the outcome the trial's row is built and its consequence runs (reward at the target, spouts, the lab's penalty sound; trial 1's comes 8 to 20 ms late, the first run of these nodes, see BACKLOG); then the timeout, and the next interval from its end (`itiStartTime`), the spouts out `drinkSeconds` into it.

The experiment folder is `<rootPath>\sub-<SubjectId>\ses-<SessionId>_<DateTime>`, with a folder per device, `Settings` (`Settings_Rig_`, `Settings_Task_`, `Settings_Experiment_`), each valve's calibration (`ValveCalibrationDO<n>`), `BonsaiData` and `manifest.json`. `BonsaiData_Trial` has one row per trial, written when the outcome is known (columns and meanings in `schemas\trial-columns.yml`); `BonsaiData_Async` one row per event outside a trial (commands, Go, each reward as `RewardDelivered` with the microlitres delivered). Status reports `trialNumber`, `trainingStage`, `target`, `trialType`, the azimuths, `rewardSizeUl` and `laserCondition`; app-rig-control computes trials, performance and water from the files. Each camera's folder (`Camera-Face`, `Camera-Pupil`, `Camera-Body`) holds an hourly `.mkv` (H.264) with a `.csv` of one row per frame: `harpTime`, `frameId`, `cameraClockNs`, `exposureUs`, `gainDb` (`LogVideoFfmpeg`; the encoder is `videoEncoder` in rig.yml).

Every file's first column is `harpTime`, the row's Harp time; a trial's moments are their own columns, in the block of columns every dome task shares (lib-open-rigs' `open_rigs.rigs.trials`, held by `tests\test_trial_columns.py`). `uv run summarise-experiment <experiment folder>` reads one experiment from its files alone: how the run ended, trials, performance, each response time and outcome-to-next-stimulus interval, and the water asked against the water delivered. It names each problem, exiting 1: trial numbers with a gap, an outcome not `Correct`, `Incorrect` or `NoResponse`, a Go or a stimulus more than 2 ms from where the task file puts it, a laser trigger more than 2 ms from `laserToStimulusSeconds` before the stimulus, an interval not starting at the last outcome plus its `penaltyTimeoutSeconds`, a timeout without the penalty sound (or the sound without one) when the task has it on, a rewarded trial with no `RewardDelivered`.

## Checking a change

`scripts\check-workflow.ps1` compiles the Extensions under C# 5, runs `tests\Extensions.Tests`, builds `src\main.bonsai` with Bonsai's own builders against the template filled in as a rig's (no window, no device), and reads each task file as at Go. `uv run --frozen --with pytest --with pyyaml --with jsonschema python -m pytest -q` runs the model and workflow tests.
