# coen-dome-decision-making

Branch `zapit-native` (not for rigs): native Zapit (ZapitDaq and the Zapit camera in the workflow, no MATLAB Zapit), on lib-open-rigs' `zapit-native` packages `0.1.1-zapit-native.1` (Devices, Trials, Logging, LiveView, Zapit) installed by hand into `local_packages`, and Bonsai.Pylon 0.4.0. At cutover the lab release re-pins them to the released `coen-lab.N`. The task files' `optogenetics.zapitConditionsPath` is the rig's mirror, `C:\Coen-Lab-Local\RigConfigs\shared\zapit-conditions\`.

The audiovisual dome decision-making tasks (localization and two modality discriminations, go/no-go and left/right), as an open-rigs project on the lab's shared blocks (lib-open-rigs, [Building Blocks](https://github.com/Coen-Lab/lib-open-rigs/blob/main/docs/articles/building-blocks.md)). It was `ucl-open/av-dome`'s `pip/main-switchover` branch, moved here with its history so production av-dome is never touched. Only this repository's sessions in the current format are read: av-dome's (the switch-over branch's included), older task files and logs are not, and a reader given an older shape fails.

The rig's hardware is in `C:\Coen-Lab-Local\RigConfigs\<MACHINE-NAME>\rig.yml`, the lab rig model `open_rigs.rigs.lab_rig.LabRig` (start from open-rigs' `rigs\rig-template.yml`).

## Running an experiment

The workflow has no window of its own. app-rig-control launches it with `--property:SubjectId=<lab ID> --property:SessionId=<n>`, and it starts into ready mode: the boards connected, the spouts homed and at that mouse's saved positions, the cameras streaming to the live view, nothing recorded. Set the parameters, send commands, then Go.

| Parameter | Default | |
|-----------|---------|-|
| `SpoutStepSize` | 150 | Motor steps per spout nudge, taken live; declared by DomeHardware's `LickSpoutStage` itself |

The task's own settings are the task file's: app-rig-control writes the file chosen at launch from `config\task*.yml`, with the Experiment page's edits, checked against `src\DataSchemas\ucl_open_coen_dome_decision_making_task.json`, at the path it passes as `TaskPath`, read at Go. From the editor `TaskPath` is `config\task-localization.yml`. `taskType` is `localization` (the answer is a side), `discriminationGoNoGo` (one spout, the left: lick on a `go` trial, withhold on a `noGo` one; a miss has no penalty; Song et al. 2017) or `discriminationLeftRight` (the left spout on a `left` trial, the right on a `right` one). The training stages are the share's Mouse Curriculum (`K:\config\curricula\coen-dome-decision-making\<task file stem>.conditions.yml`, edited on app-rig-control's Mouse Curriculum page): at launch app-rig-control writes the chosen stage's values over the task file's (`trialConditions` set whole; a field the stage leaves unset keeps the file's) and fills `curriculum` (`id`, `code`, `version`, `path`, `label`, `sha256`), the only record of the stage (the workflow has no stage parameter): Go refuses a `curriculum` that is empty or malformed, and `TaskFile` reports its `code-version` (S03A-k7f2) as the metric `curriculumStage`. The repo's task files hold the defaults (the first stage's values) and an empty `curriculum`. The trial mix is `trialConditions`, each `{weight, pairs, set}`: each trial picks a condition by weight and draws the task's values with its `set` over them. What a trial shows and rewards are its settings, never its trialType: `visualStimulus.initialContrasts` (0 no grating: logged `visualInitialContrast` 0 with `visualInitialAzimuthDeg` NaN, as the sound's when none plays), `auditoryStimulus.initialLevelDbfs` (dBFS RMS of the waveform set as one, one gain for all 100 files; `mute` no sound) at a pick of `auditoryStimulus.initialAzimuthsDeg` on rig.yml's speaker nearest it at `auditoryStimulus.initialElevationDeg` (0 the centre; `"all"` every rig.yml speaker at once, which has no side, so target `auditorySide` refuses it as it does 0, and is logged at `auditoryInitialAzimuthDeg` NaN with its `auditoryInitialLevelDbfs`), `target` (each condition's own, never the task's: `visualSide`, `auditorySide`, `left`, `right`, `go`, `noGo`, `random`, `noReward`), `repeats.maxRepeats`/`repeatOn` (`never` exactly when `maxRepeats` is 0), `spouts.alwaysIn` (the used spouts always in on that trial: no withdrawal, no single-spout move), `spouts.singleSpoutProbability` (only the target's spout comes in, on a scored localization or left-right trial whose spouts move), `penalty.<kind>.sound.levelDbfs` and with optogenetics `optogenetics.laserDurationSeconds`; the grating's shape (`visualStimulus.initialElevationDeg`, `initialSigmaDeg`, `initialOrientationDeg`, `initialSpatialFrequencyCpd`, `initialTemporalFrequencyHz`) is read from the trial's combination too. `pairs` makes lists share one pick (coherent: the grating and sound azimuths together; conflict: mirrored). `trialType` names the condition (logged as `trialType`), derived by the schema's `x-trial-types` at Go and never stored in a task file. The schema's `x-session-fields` lists what a condition may not set (`rngSeed`, `curriculum`, `trialConditions`, `taskType`, `optogenetics.enabled` and `optogenetics.zapitConditionsPath`; task.py `SESSION_FIELDS` gives each one's reason): every other field a condition may set, read per trial from the trial's combination, in which every list field (`visualStimulus.initialContrasts`, the initial azimuths) is one entry, each combination a row of the experiment's `Settings\Settings_Combinations_<time>.json` (written at Go: its `trialConditionId`, trialType, weight and the values that differ between combinations or from `Settings_Task`), logged as the trial's `trialCombinationId`; `x-max-combinations` caps a condition's combinations (1024), `x-required-conditions` says what some weighted condition must draw (a go/no-go task a `go` target) and `x-rules` the rules linking settings (a task type allows only its targets; a probe repeats `never` or on `noResponse`, never `notCorrect`), JSON Schema over dotted paths checked by lib-open-rigs' `open_rigs.rigs.conditions` in pydantic, at check-workflow and by app-rig-control at launch; Go checks what needs the rig, and again each condition's target and a probe's repeat rule. A trial with no right answer is a probe (outcome `probe`, left out of performance), repeated only after a miss when its `repeatOn` is `noResponse`: target `noReward` (never rewarded or penalised; in localization and left-right both spouts move in, `rewardOfferedUl` 0, the task files' default probe), or target `random` in localization and left-right: both spouts move in and a choice at either is rewarded there on a coin drawn with the trial from the seeded stream (heads), else unrewarded. A random probe is penalised only when its condition's `penaliseRandomProbes` is true (default false): then tails as an incorrect choice and a miss that was not auto-rewarded as `penalty.noResponse`. A `noReward` probe is never rewarded or penalised. A go/no-go task with no weighted `go` target is refused. A repeated trial never has the laser on.

Commands, while ready or recording: `RewardValve-Left.Reward` and `RewardValve-Right.Reward`, each 2.5 µl on that spout through the valve's calibration (a fixed manual drop, each `RewardValve` piece's), and `LickSpoutStage`'s own (lib-open-rigs'): `SpoutsHome`, `SpoutsIn`, `SpoutsOut`, `SaveSpoutPositions` (the mouse's row of `RigConfigs\<RIG>\LickSpoutPositions.csv`, beside rig.yml) and the nudges `LeftUp`, `LeftDown`, `RightUp`, `RightDown`, `LeftIn`, `LeftOut`, `RightIn`, `RightOut`, `BaseLeft`, `BaseRight`; a nudge moves the spouts at once and changes that mouse's in positions, which `SaveSpoutPositions` keeps for its next experiment.

## A trial

The trial is one state machine on the Harp clock, lib-open-rigs' `TrialLoop` (the same as coen-dome-navigation's), running this task's tested C# rules (`TrialRules` in `TrialLogic.cs`); `TaskLogic` sends each of its events to the hardware. Go reads the task file and checks it once (`CheckTaskAtGo`, in `TaskFile`, building `TrialRules`: a well formed `curriculum`, the weights and repeat rules keyed for the task type and a go/no-go stage with Go trials, the lab's spouts and stimulus-off rules (OpenRigs.Trials `Spouts.Problems`, `StimulusTiming.Problems`: `spouts.rewardConsumptionSeconds` plus the spouts' lead before the stimulus within `itiMinSeconds`, the stimulus off before the next trial's earliest stimulus), each penalty kind's values (`penalty.noResponse` null exactly in go/no-go, `Penalties.Problems`), the reward tone mute, `laserToStimulusSeconds` within the shortest interval less Zapit's 0.2 s reply margin (`ZapitTrial.ReplyMarginSeconds`) and no later than the response window, the quiet waits, and with optogenetics a `laserDurationSeconds` short enough for Zapit to be armed for the next trial, and the conditions file), stopping the workflow naming each problem; once the experiment folder exists the spouts are drawn back (unless trial 1's spouts stay in) and the loop starts (`RunTrials`), the first trial's interval starting then. Each trial is drawn at the start of its interval, the last trial's end plus its timeout (`TrialDraw`: a condition by the weights, then one of its combinations; repeats by each combination's rules, the speaker that plays is rig.yml's nearest to the drawn location, or every speaker at `"all"`, its own `itiSeconds`, and with optogenetics a Zapit condition on every trial, the laser off at 0 mW on laser-off trials). It starts at the interval's end less the lead, max(`laserToStimulusSeconds`, 0), or later until the quiet waits hold (`trialStructure.lickQuietSeconds` with both spouts released, a held contact holding the start; the wheel's travel, forward and back added, within `wheelQuietCm` over the last `wheelQuietSeconds`; each counted from the interval's start; all 0, off, in the task files); `Trial` and the status metrics change then. The stimulus comes the lead after the trial start, in every experiment, so with a quiet mouse it lands exactly `itiSeconds` after the interval's start (`quietWaitSeconds` says how much the waits added). With optogenetics, `ZapitTrials` sends a hardware-triggered Zapit the trial's StartStimulus during the interval (`Arm`, or once the last trial's laser and its 0.5 s maximum ramp-down have ended; a rig without hardware triggering, test mouse only, gets it at the trigger, as Zapit starts the laser on receipt), the loop checks the reply, and the ZapitTrigger line (Zapit's trigger and the masking light) rises at the stimulus less `laserToStimulusSeconds` (signed: before, with or after the stimulus) and falls `laserDurationSeconds` later, unless the trial was answered first (no laser, and the row logs `laserPowerMw` 0); on a hardware-triggered rig a reply not in by then stops the workflow, so Zapit adds no time. The stimulus (`stimulusOnsetTime`, the first clock tick at or after it was due), the grating and the sound pulsing together from it, a 50 ms pulse every 1/`stimulus.rateHz` s, until the trial loop's `StimulusOff` event (OpenRigs.Trials `StimulusTiming`: the earlier of `stimulus.durationSeconds` after it and `stimulus.responseToStimulusOffSeconds` after the outcome, a miss's at the response window's end; once a trial, `stimulusOffTime`) and the spouts in `spouts.stimulusToSpoutsInSeconds` after it, before it when negative, the trial then starting that much earlier (`SpoutsIn`: both, or the target's alone on a `singleSpoutOffered` trial; never after the outcome, as the response window opens no sooner, `stimulusToResponseWindowSeconds` at least a positive `spouts.stimulusToSpoutsInSeconds`); the response window `stimulusToResponseWindowSeconds` after it for `responseWindowSeconds`: each lick in it adds 1 (left) or -1 (right) to the net licks, the grating moving `azimuthShiftPerLickDeg` a lick from its drawn azimuth, and the choice is `left` at `thresholdLicks`, `right` at -`thresholdLicks`, else `noResponse` at the window's end (in `discriminationGoNoGo` only left licks count and the choice is `go` at `thresholdLicks`, so a correct go trial's choice is its target); a lick before the window is not counted. The answer is the trial's target: `left` or `right`, in go/no-go `go` or `noGo`, or on a probe `noReward` or `random` (`schemas\trial-columns.yml`). At the outcome the trial's row is built and its consequence runs from the row (its `rewarded`: `rewardOfferedUl` at the target, or a random trial's chosen spout, the other spout drawn back; its `penalised`: both spouts drawn back, the lab's penalty sound at the trial's `penalty.<kind>.sound.levelDbfs`, none at `mute`, and the timeout; a trial whose spouts are always in moves none); the trial then runs on to the response window's end whatever the choice, so it lasts the same whatever the mouse does (`trialStructure.responseWindowAlwaysFull`, fixed true; `trialEndTime`); then the timeout when penalised, and the next interval from its end (`itiStartTime`), the spouts out `spouts.rewardConsumptionSeconds` into it (`SpoutsOut`) unless the trial drawn at that interval's start has its spouts always in (the loop draws it before the cue, at 0 s too). Every spout move is lib-open-rigs' `SpoutCommands`.

## Data

The experiment folder is `<rootPath>\sub-<SubjectId>\ses-<SessionId>_<DateTime>`, with a folder per device, `Settings` (`Settings_Rig_`, `Settings_Task_`, `Settings_Combinations_`, `Settings_Experiment_`), each valve's calibration under `RewardValve-Left\` and `RewardValve-Right\`, `WorkflowData` and `manifest.json`. `WorkflowData_Trials` has one row per trial, written when the outcome is known (columns and meanings in `schemas\trial-columns.yml`); `WorkflowData_Events` one row per event outside a trial (commands, Go, each reward as `RewardDelivered` with the microlitres delivered). Status reports `trialNumber`, `curriculumStage`, `target`, `trialType`, the azimuths (`visualInitialAzimuthDeg`, `auditoryInitialAzimuthDeg`), `rewardOfferedUl` and `laserCondition`; app-rig-control computes trials, performance and water from the files. Each camera's folder (`Camera-Face`, `Camera-Pupil`, `Camera-Body`) holds an hourly `.mkv` (H.264) with a `.csv` of one row per frame: `harpTime`, `frameId`, `cameraClockNs`, `exposureUs`, `gainDb` (`LogVideoFfmpeg`; the encoder is `videoEncoder` in rig.yml).

Every file's first column is `harpTime`, the row's Harp time; a trial's moments are their own columns, in the block of columns every dome task shares (lib-open-rigs' `open_rigs.rigs.trials`, held by `tests\test_trial_columns.py`). `uv run summarise-experiment <experiment folder>` reads one experiment from its files alone: how the run ended, trials, performance, each response time and trial-end-to-next-stimulus interval, and the water asked against the water delivered. It names each problem, exiting 1: trial numbers with a gap, an outcome not `correct`, `incorrect`, `noResponse`, `probe` or `auto`, a trial start or a stimulus more than 2 ms from where the task file puts it, a laser trigger more than 2 ms from `laserToStimulusSeconds` before the stimulus, an interval not starting at the last trial's end (`trialEndTime`, never before its outcome) plus its `penaltyTimeoutSeconds`, a `penalised` trial without the task's timeout (or an unpenalised one with it), a `rewarded` trial with no `RewardDelivered` at its outcome requesting its `rewardOfferedUl`, or a `RewardDelivered` at the outcome of a trial not rewarded.
