## Decided

- The hardware is lib-open-rigs' `DomeHardware`; this workflow keeps its own task file reading, task C# (`src\Extensions`, tested in `tests\Extensions.Tests`, every wait on the Harp clock) and auditory stimuli (`src\sounds`, checked against the mixer's rate at Start). What every task does the same way is lib's: the trial's state machine (`TrialLoop`, as coen-dome-navigation's, the task's own part its `TrialRules`; 16 Nov 2026) and the includes (`LaunchSettings`, `BackgroundNoise`, `PlayPenaltySound`, `TaskLogs`, `LogRngSeed`, `Rewards`, `ExperimentRecords`, `ConnectForTask`); the pieces declare their own commands and parameters, so ExperimentControl declares none.
- The background noise is the lab's one file, rig.yml's `speakerArray.backgroundNoise` (`../shared/sounds/background_noise.wav`, coen-dome-navigation's float white noise; Pip, 16 Nov 2026, accepted its spectrum against the old file's: 0.5 dB lower to 19 kHz, +1.3 dB over 16-24 kHz), from Ready, faded in over 2 s, at the task file's level as launched; Go stops if the form changed `backgroundNoise`.
- Every sound's level: the stimulus at `auditoryStimulus.amplitude` (the trial-type split, 18 Nov 2026: 1 the WAV as stored, -47.2 dBFS RMS, production av-dome's unscaled loudness; above 1 allowed, the -1 dBFS peak after equalisation checked at Go at the largest amplitude any condition can draw, naming the condition and speaker; the failure's last words are lib-open-rigs' `SoundLevel.CheckPeak`'s, "lower its levelDbfs"), the background noise and penalty sound at their `levelDbfs` (dBFS RMS as stored, before equalisation): background -60.1, penalty -44.6, the levels played before 15 Nov 2026. The penalty sound is the lab's, rig.yml's `speakerArray.penaltySound` (`../shared/sounds/penalty_tone.wav`), on rig.yml's `penaltySoundChannel`, at 48 kHz as every sound (13 Nov 2026).
- The quiet waits are `trialStructure`'s (`lickQuietSeconds`, `wheelQuietSeconds`, `wheelQuietCm`, 0 in every task file), capped at 10 s, 10 s and 20 cm; the wheel's counts travel in the last `wheelQuietSeconds`, the lick's needs both spouts released.
- Zapit (Pip, 10-14 Nov 2026): it adds no time; `laserToStimulusSeconds` is signed and its lead applies in every experiment; each trial is drawn, and Zapit armed, at the start of its interval, and a reply not in by the trigger stops the workflow; the ZapitTrigger line (DO3) is a held level for `laserDurationSeconds`, never board-pulsed (`outputPulseEnable: "DO1, DO2"`); the laser is timed, whatever the mouse does; no laser after an answer (`laserPowerMw` 0); any Zapit error stops the experiment; real mice need a hardware-triggered rig.
- Discrimination split into `discriminationGoNoGo` and `discriminationLeftRight` (Pip, 16 Nov 2026): go/no-go scored as Song et al. 2017 (one spout, the left; a lick on Go rewarded, on NoGo penalised; a miss and a withheld NoGo neither; a two-modality or neutral trial a Probe, never rewarded, penalised or repeated (Pip); `rewardOfferedUl` 0 where no reward is on offer); left/right's two-modality and neutral trials random-reward probes (Pip: "randomly rewarded"; next item); `targetModality` became `goModality` and `leftModality`, as in left/right both modalities are answers. The values are camelCase: Bonsai's YAML reader ignores enum aliases, so `discrimination-go-no-go` failed at Go. A mouse's earlier `discrimination` experiments suggest no stage (another task type): choose it by hand.
- Trial-type split (Pip, 18 Nov 2026; plan trial-type-split): a condition is `{label, weight, pairs, set}`; `type` drove the draw and is gone. What a trial shows and rewards are condition fields (`x-conditionFields`, every one settable, a blank taking the stage's then the task's value): `contrasts` (0 no grating), `auditoryStimulus.amplitude` (0 no sound; replaces `auditoryStimulus.levelDbfs`) and `auditoryStimulus.initialAzimuthsDeg` (beside the grating's; 0 the centre; the nearest speaker plays), `target` (localization `visualSide`, `auditorySide`, `random`; go/no-go `go`, `noGo`, `noReward`; left/right `left`, `right`, `random`; logged as before: Left, Right, Go, NoGo, NoReward, Random), `repeats.maxRepeats` and `repeats.repeatOn` (`never`, `noResponse`, `notCorrect`). `goModality`, `leftModality`, `allowRepeats` and `rulesPerType` are gone: an auditory-Go mouse gets its own task file. `pairs` zips equally long lists (coherent: grating and sound azimuths [-60, 60] with [-60, 60]; conflict with [60, -60]); every other list cell is picked independently; a list field's entries are one combination each. The rules linking settings are one table (task.py `RULES`, the schema's `x-rules`, C# `TrialDraw.Rules`, a test holding them equal), checked on the task and every combination by pydantic and at Go: `maxRepeats` 0 -> `repeatOn` never; `target` random -> `bothSpoutsProbability` 1 and `repeatOn` never; `noReward` -> `repeatOn` never; `taskType` -> the targets above; contrasts all 0 or amplitude 0 -> that stimulus's azimuths unused. `label` (visual, auditory, audiovisualCoherent, audiovisualConflict, neutral, go, noGo, left, right; task.py `derive_label` derives it) is logged as `trialType` and drives nothing; a new `auditoryAmplitude` column. The repo's task files draw what each old type drew (TrialDrawTests.TheConvertedFilesDrawWhatEachOldTrialTypeDrew); their repeats stay off (`repeatOn` never), as `allowRepeats` false was.
- Sound on every speaker and no `auditoryOn` (Pip, 18 Nov 2026): an `auditoryStimulus.initialAzimuthsDeg` item may be `"all"` ("Position in general should accept an input for All meaning all connected speakers"), playing the sound on every rig.yml speaker at once, each its own equalised copy (so the Go peak check of every speaker covers it); a number is still the nearest speaker. It has no side: target `auditorySide` refuses it as it does 0, and `derive_label` counts it as no cue. Logged at `auditoryAzimuthDeg` and `auditoryElevationDeg` NaN with `auditoryAmplitude` above 0 (silent: NaN with 0); the C# `Trial.speakerChannel` is `Trial.AllSpeakersChannel` (-2), -1 silent. The schema's item is `oneOf` number or `"all"` (pydantic's `anyOf` makes Bonsai.Sgen an empty class; `oneOf` gives `List<object>`, read by `TrialDraw.SoundAzimuth`). The trial's `auditoryOn` is gone ("Just have amplitude"): `auditoryAmplitude` 0 is no sound and `visualContrast` 0 no grating; it was never a column, so no old data needs it.
- Trial conditions (Pip, 17 Nov 2026; plan trial-conditions v0.5): `trialWeights` became `trialConditions`, a list of `{type, weight, set}`, one whole value a curriculum stage's conditions replace (never carried, never indexed; the schema's `x-whole`). `set` holds condition-level values by dotted path, only the fields in the schema's `x-conditionFields` for the types each applies to (`contrasts`, `visualStimulus.initialAzimuthsDeg`, `visualStimulus.elevationDeg`, `optogenetics.laserOnProbability` (optogenetics on), `reward.rewardSizeUl`, `autoRewardProbability`, `bothSpoutsProbability`); a list in a scalar field's cell is one pick per trial, each condition expanding into at most 64 combinations, each checked as a task at Go (TrialDraw) and by pydantic. The trial row's `trialCondition` is the condition's index. Positions: `visualStimulus.initialAzimuthsDeg` (signed, never 0; the picked one's sign is the side and, in localization's visual, auditory and coherent trials, the target) and `elevationDeg`; the grating starts there exactly, the sound plays on the speaker nearest it, a conflict trial's at the mirrored azimuth; `azimuthMin/MaxDeg`, `fixedAzimuthsDeg`, `elevationMin/MaxDeg`, `coherentAzimuthCapDeg`, `speakerLocations` and the side coin are gone. Before that (16 Nov 2026), trial mix by role, per mouse: `trialWeights` was top-level, per mouse and carried, a curriculum stage that sets it setting the whole map (the last stages leave it, the earlier set their shaping mixes); the discriminations key it by role (`go`, `noGo` or `left`, `right`, with `audiovisualCoherent`, `audiovisualConflict`, `neutral`), `goModality` or `leftModality` filling the role, so an auditory mouse needs no swapped weights and a stage advance keeps working; localization keys it by stimulus. Only a trial with a right answer repeats: `rulesPerType` is `visual`, `auditory`, `audiovisualCoherent` in localization, `go`, `noGo` and `left`, `right`, any other key refused. A trial with no right answer is a Probe: go/no-go's pay nothing; localization's `audiovisualConflict` and `neutral` and left-right's two-modality and `neutral` are random-reward probes, both spouts in, a choice rewarded there or penalised on a coin drawn with the trial, a miss as a scored miss; its `rewardOfferedUl` is the stage's, heads or tails, and `rewarded` says whether it paid. lib-open-rigs' summarise-experiment counts a paid probe in both the water asked (`rewardOfferedUl` of each `rewarded` trial) and RewardDelivered.
