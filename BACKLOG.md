## Decided

- The response window always runs in full (Pip, 20 Nov 2026; OpenRigs.Trials coen-lab.16): `trialStructure.responseWindowAlwaysFull`, lib-open-rigs' ResponseWindowAlwaysFull block, fixed true (a `Literal[True]` frozen field; false is refused in Python and at Go, so no stage, condition or form can change it). A lick still gives the outcome at once (reward, spouts, penalty sound and flash), but the trial ends at the window's end, so its timing is the same whatever the mouse does and licking on a probe or a noGo trial buys no time. The trial row gains the lab's trialEnd block (`trialEndTime`, after `outcomeTime`); the next interval starts at `trialEndTime` plus the timeout. The shared `itiStartTime` description still says outcomeTime (lib-open-rigs' word for word); this task's note says trialEndTime. The Go and model timing checks keep the outcome as the soonest end, which only leaves room to spare. Older recordings converted by jacuzzi-ingest carry `trialEndTime` = `outcomeTime`, exact for them.
- `penaliseProbes` renamed `penaliseRandomProbes` (Pip, 20 Nov 2026; no old name kept): it only ever affected random-reward probes (tails and a miss penalised when true, default false); a noReward probe is never rewarded or penalised. app-rig-control's views and the share's curricula follow.
- Initial names (Pip, 20 Nov 2026): every stimulus setting says it is the value as the trial starts, since any may one day change within a trial. In the task file `visualStimulus.contrasts`, `elevationDeg`, `sigmaDeg`, `orientationDeg`, `spatialFrequencyCpd` and `temporalFrequencyHz` are `initialContrasts`, `initialElevationDeg`, `initialSigmaDeg`, `initialOrientationDeg`, `initialSpatialFrequencyCpd` and `initialTemporalFrequencyHz`, and `auditoryStimulus.levelDbfs` and `elevationDeg` are `initialLevelDbfs` and `initialElevationDeg` (lib-open-rigs' `ct.AuditoryStimulus`). Trial columns put the modality first, then the timing word: `visualInitialContrast`, `auditoryInitialLevelDbfs`, `auditoryInitialElevationDeg`. A clean break: old keys are refused; jacuzzi-ingest maps old names when converting recorded data. The list fields are drawn in path order, so `visualStimulus.initialContrasts` now comes after `initialAzimuthsDeg`: a task with several of each numbers its combinations (trialCombinationId) in a new order, and a seed draws other trials than before.
- The spouts and the stimulus off are lib-open-rigs' (Pip, 20 Nov 2026; OpenRigs.Trials coen-lab.11, no old names kept): the `spouts` block (`alwaysIn`, was the condition-level `spoutsStayIn`, still settable per condition; `stimulusToSpoutsInSeconds`, was `trialStructure.stimulusToSpoutsInSeconds`, now signed, negative bringing the spouts in before the stimulus and the trial start that much earlier; `rewardConsumptionSeconds`, was `trialStructure.outcomeToSpoutsOutSeconds`; this task's `singleSpoutProbability`, was top-level), moved by lib-open-rigs' `SpoutCommands` on the trials' `ISpoutTrial`; `stimulus.responseToStimulusOffSeconds` (was `endsAtResponse`: false is null, true is 0) with `stimulus.durationSeconds` (may be null, not both), the trial loop's one `StimulusOff` event a trial hiding the grating and stopping the sound, and `stimulusOffTime` a lab core column after `stimulusOnsetTime`.
- The hardware is lib-open-rigs' `DomeHardware`; this workflow keeps its own task file reading, task C# (`src\Extensions`, tested in `tests\Extensions.Tests`, every wait on the Harp clock) and auditory stimuli (`src\sounds`, checked against the mixer's rate at Start). What every task does the same way is lib's: the trial's state machine (`TrialLoop`, as coen-dome-navigation's, the task's own part its `TrialRules`; 16 Nov 2026) and the includes (`LaunchSettings`, `BackgroundNoise`, `PlayPenaltySound`, `TaskLogs`, `LogRngSeed`, `Rewards`, `ExperimentRecords`, `ConnectForTask`); the pieces declare their own commands and parameters, so ExperimentControl declares none.
- The background noise is the lab's one file, rig.yml's `speakerArray.backgroundNoise` (`../shared/sounds/background_noise.wav`, coen-dome-navigation's float white noise; Pip, 16 Nov 2026, accepted its spectrum against the old file's: 0.5 dB lower to 19 kHz, +1.3 dB over 16-24 kHz), from Ready, faded in over 2 s, at the lab's -60 dBFS (dome cleanup P15, 19 Nov 2026: no task setting).
- Dome cleanup (b)+(c) (Pip, 18-19 Nov 2026; plan-dome-cleanup, inventory-decisions P1-P19): no old names kept. Fields `visualStimulus.contrasts`, `visualStimulus.sigmaDeg`, `trialStructure.visualStimulusSeconds`, `trialStructure.outcomeToSpoutsOutSeconds` (cue `SpoutsOut`), `response.azimuthShiftPerLickDeg`, `reward.sizeUl`/`largeMultiplier`/`largeProbability`, `auditoryStimulus.levelDbfs` (`mute` or dBFS, the set as one gain) and `elevationDeg`, `singleSpoutProbability` (1 - the old bothSpoutsProbability), `spoutsStayIn` (the old spoutMovements inverted, per trial); `target` is each condition's only (P10); `repeatOn: never` exactly when `maxRepeats: 0`, a probe repeats never or on `noResponse` (P3, P6); the grating's shape is read from the trial's combination (P13); session fields only rngSeed, curriculum, trialConditions, taskType and Zapit's set-up (P19); columns `trialConditionId`, `auditoryLevelDbfs`, `auditoryInitialAzimuthDeg`, `visualInitialAzimuthDeg`, `autoReward`, `singleSpoutOffered`, words camelCase.

- Discrimination split into `discriminationGoNoGo` and `discriminationLeftRight` (Pip, 16 Nov 2026): go/no-go scored as Song et al. 2017 (one spout, the left; a lick on Go rewarded, on NoGo penalised; a miss and a withheld NoGo neither; a two-modality or neutral trial a Probe, never rewarded, penalised or repeated (Pip); `rewardOfferedUl` 0 where no reward is on offer); left/right's two-modality and neutral trials random-reward probes (Pip: "randomly rewarded"; next item); `targetModality` became `goModality` and `leftModality`, as in left/right both modalities are answers. What a trial shows and rewards are fields a condition's `set` may hold (any but `x-session-fields`, a blank taking the stage's then the task's value): `contrasts` (0 no grating), `auditoryStimulus.amplitude` (0 no sound) and `auditoryStimulus.initialAzimuthsDeg` (beside the grating's; 0 the centre; the nearest speaker plays), `target` (localization `visualSide`, `auditorySide`, `random`; go/no-go `go`, `noGo`, `noReward`; left/right `left`, `right`, `random`; logged as Left, Right, Go, NoGo, NoReward, Random), `repeats.maxRepeats` and `repeats.repeatOn` (`never`, `noResponse`, `notCorrect`). An auditory-Go mouse gets its own task file. `pairs` zips equally long lists (coherent: grating and sound azimuths [-60, 60] with [-60, 60]; conflict with [60, -60]); every other list cell is picked independently; a list field's entries are one combination each. The rules linking settings are one table (task.py `RULES`, the schema's `x-rules`, C# `TrialDraw.Rules`, a test holding them equal), checked on the task and every combination by pydantic and at Go: `maxRepeats` 0 -> `repeatOn` never; `target` random -> `bothSpoutsProbability` 1 and `repeatOn` never; `noReward` -> `repeatOn` never; `taskType` -> the targets above; contrasts all 0 or amplitude 0 -> that stimulus's azimuths unused. `trialType` (visual, auditory, audiovisualCoherent, audiovisualConflict, neutral, go, noGo, left, right; task.py `derive_trial_type` derives it) is logged and drives nothing. The repo's task files keep repeats off (`repeatOn` never).
