## Decided

- The hardware is lib-open-rigs' `DomeHardware`; this workflow keeps its own task file reading, task C# (`src\Extensions`, tested in `tests\Extensions.Tests`, every wait on the Harp clock) and auditory stimuli (`src\LocalFiles\AuditoryStimuli`, checked against the mixer's rate at Start). What every task does the same way is lib's: the trial's state machine (`TrialLoop`, as coen-dome-navigation's, the task's own part its `TrialRules`; 16 Nov 2026) and the includes (`LaunchSettings`, `TrainingStage`, `BackgroundNoise`, `PlayPenaltySound`, `TaskLogs`, `LogRngSeed`, `BehaviorBoard.Rewards`, `ExperimentRecords`, `Zapit.ConnectForTask`); the pieces declare their own commands and parameters, so ExperimentControl declares only `TrainingStage`.
- The background noise is the lab's one file, rig.yml's `speakerArray.backgroundNoise` (`../shared/sounds/background_noise.wav`, coen-dome-navigation's float white noise; Pip, 16 Nov 2026, accepted its spectrum against the old file's: 0.5 dB lower to 19 kHz, +1.3 dB over 16-24 kHz), from Ready, faded in over 2 s, at the task file's level as launched; Go stops if the form changed `backgroundNoise`.
- Every sound's level is the task file's `levelDbfs` (dBFS RMS as stored, before equalisation): stimuli -47.2, background -60.1, penalty -44.6, the levels played before 15 Nov 2026. The penalty sound is the lab's, on rig.yml's `penaltySoundChannel`, at 48 kHz as every sound (13 Nov 2026).
- The quiet waits are per stage (`lickQuietSeconds`, `wheelQuietSeconds`, `wheelQuietCm`, 0 in every stage), capped at 10 s, 10 s and 20 cm; the wheel's counts travel in the last `wheelQuietSeconds`, the lick's needs both spouts released.
- Zapit (Pip, 10-14 Nov 2026): it adds no time; `laserToStimulusSeconds` is signed and its lead applies in every experiment; each trial is drawn, and Zapit armed, at the start of its interval, and a reply not in by the trigger stops the workflow; the ZapitTrigger line (DO3) is a held level for `laserDurationSeconds`, never board-pulsed (`outputPulseEnable: "DO1, DO2"`); the laser is timed, whatever the mouse does; no laser after an answer (`laserPowerMw` 0); any Zapit error stops the experiment; real mice need a hardware-triggered rig.
- Discrimination split into `discriminationGoNoGo` and `discriminationLeftRight` (Pip, 16 Nov 2026): go/no-go scored as Song et al. 2017 (one spout, the left; a lick on Go rewarded, on NoGo penalised; a miss and a withheld NoGo neither; a two-modality or neutral trial a Probe, never rewarded, penalised or repeated (Pip); `rewardSizeUl` 0 where no reward is on offer); left/right with a two-modality trial's side drawn at random, apart from the stimuli's (Pip: "randomly rewarded"); `targetModality` became `goModality` and `leftModality`, as in left/right both modalities are answers. The values are camelCase: Bonsai's YAML reader ignores enum aliases, so `discrimination-go-no-go` failed at Go. A mouse's earlier `discrimination` experiments suggest no stage (another task type): choose it by hand.
- Trial mix by role, per mouse (Pip, 16 Nov 2026): `trialWeights` is top-level, per mouse and carried, a stage's own replacing it for that stage (the last stages take it, the earlier keep their shaping mixes); the discriminations key it by role (`go`, `noGo` or `left`, `right`, with `audiovisualCoherent`, `audiovisualConflict`, `neutral`), `goModality` or `leftModality` filling the role, so an auditory mouse needs no swapped weights and a stage advance keeps working; localization keys it by stimulus. Only a trial with a right answer repeats: `rulesPerType` is `visual`, `auditory`, `audiovisualCoherent` in localization, `go`, `noGo` and `left`, `right`, any other key refused. A trial with no right answer is a Probe: go/no-go's pay nothing; localization's `audiovisualConflict` and `neutral` and left-right's two-modality and `neutral` are random-reward probes, both spouts in, a choice rewarded there or penalised on a coin drawn with the trial, a miss as a scored miss, `rewardSizeUl` above 0 only when it paid. lib-open-rigs' summarise-experiment counts a paid probe in RewardDelivered but not in "ul asked by the task".
- Discrimination's interval matches Gerion's sessions (Pip, 13 Nov 2026): `itiMinSeconds` 1.5, `itiMaxSeconds` 2.0, `drinkSeconds` 1.5; trial 1 has an interval too.
- Auto-reward is a flag with outcome `NoResponse`; contrasts, coherent sides and both spouts as Gerion's; trial types drawn independently (Pip).
- Every reward goes through lib-open-rigs' `DeliverReward`, which stops on a reward line that is a fixed pulse or has no valve calibration.
- The Pupil camera's gain stays 20 until a better lens arrives (Pip, 14 Nov 2026); re-measure frame size and writer load then.
- swc-aeon is open-rigs' (lib-open-rigs), never pinned here; BSD 3-Clause, as lib-open-rigs.
- Recorded names changed (Pip, 16 Nov 2026; one name per meaning across the two dome tasks): the task file's `trialStructure.openLoopSeconds` is `responseDelaySeconds` and `closedLoopSeconds` is `responseWindowSeconds` (in Settings_Task), as coen-dome-navigation's per-stage `responseWindowSeconds` and lib-open-rigs' `TrialLoop` (response delay, response window); the `repeats.rulesPerType` value `onTimeout` is `onNoResponse` (it repeats after a NoResponse), since timeout means only the penalty's (`penalty.timeoutSeconds`, `penaltyTimeoutSeconds`). No trial column changed; earlier experiments keep the old names in their Settings_Task. In code, `TrialRow.TimedOut` is `Penalised` and the Audio group's `PlayStim` is `PlayAuditoryStimulus`.
- Not changed on audit: `outcomeTime` duplicates `harpTime` so every moment is named; trial-type names differ from navigation's as the tasks differ; the response window is in `trialStructure` here (one for every stage) and per stage in navigation (null on its first stages); `laserDurationSeconds` (a timed stimulus) and navigation's `laserMaxSeconds` (a cap) differ as the lasers do, as lib-open-rigs' `LaserTiming`; `taskType` (what the mouse answers) and navigation's `modality` (what every trial shows) are different settings, and `goModality` and `leftModality` take the same modality words; the columns `audioAzimuthDeg`, `audioElevationDeg` and `audioWaveformIndex` and the task file's `audioStimulus` keep the `audio` words production av-dome records (app-rig-control's av-dome view shows its `audioAzimuthDeg`), as renaming them here alone would give the lab two names for each, though the modality is `auditory` (trial types, `goModality`, `leftModality`); `rngSeed` in the task file is what was asked, the `RngSeed` event what ran; a trial cut by Stop has no row; the response window, spouts in, stimulus off and Go time follow from the task file and the row's columns; internal Trial fields keep old names (only recorded names, task fields and metrics are the lab's).
